# VeriQuote Marketing & Sales Notes

## Positioning

"Seal the job. End the dispute."

Local-first quoting that produces a client-verifiable QuantumGuard evidence bundle. Works offline. Pairs with LedgerLite invoices and Chaser follow-ups.

## Target buyers

- Roofers, HVAC, plumbers, electricians, landscapers
- Independent operators and small trade companies
- Anyone who has lost a claim because "there was no proof"

## Outreach hooks

1. "Your quote is now evidence. Client can verify it offline with free QuantumGuard."
2. "Stop fighting about what was agreed. Seal it."
3. "Pay once. No subscription. Runs on your laptop."
